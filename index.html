Honest dimsum rooted in family hospitality.

What began as a humble home kitchen sharing warm bamboo baskets with neighbors in Medan has grown into Dimsum Ummu Furqon – an artisanal culinary house dedicated to clean, deeply comforting Cantonese-Indonesian dimsum.

Unlike mass-produced frozen dimsum laden with flour, we maintain an 85% pure meat-and-seafood ratio in every fold, coarsely minced for springy texture and seasoned with toasted sesame oil, white pepper, and ginger broth.
